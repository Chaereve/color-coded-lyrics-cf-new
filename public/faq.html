FAQ & How It Works

Last updated: 29/09/2026 · Back to the request board

This page explains how the request board works, in the order people usually ask. Every number here is the same one the app uses — nothing is rounded for marketing.

What is this page?

This is the community request board for @chaereve's colour-coded lyric videos. You can request songs (colour-coded lyrics, full albums, 1-hour loops and Shorts), vote for what other people requested, follow a song's progress from queue to finished video, and see where you stand on the leaderboard.

How do requests work?

Sign in with Google, press New request, and pick a type:

  • Free request — up to 3 per hour. It goes to pending until an admin approves it, then it joins the public queue and everyone can vote for it.
  • Paid request — $0.75 / 20.000đ. Approved as soon as payment is confirmed: it skips voting entirely and goes to the front of the queue. Paid requests don't count against the 3-per-hour limit — send as many as you like.

Before you send, the form checks whether the song is already on the board and offers to open its vote box instead — the same request split across three people is how votes get wasted. Your draft is saved in the browser while you type, so closing the form by accident loses nothing.

How many free votes do I get, and what do extra votes cost?

Everyone gets 3 free votes per day, resetting at midnight (Vietnam time). You can put many votes on the same song — the vote button opens a box where you choose the amount, and withdrawing votes gives them back to your balance.

How is the next song picked — and when will mine be made?

Automatically, one song per cycle (the countdown on the board shows the next pick). The order is fixed: a paid request goes first, otherwise the queued song with the most votes, with ties going to the oldest request.

The wait shown on your request — “at least N days/weeks” — is the earliest it could happen: time to the next pick, plus one cycle for each song ahead of yours. More votes for another song can push yours later, never earlier. Every request on the board shows this estimate and how far it is from being picked.

What happens after a song is picked?

Picked songs move through picked → in progress → completed. You can follow any request to get a browser notification at each stage, and completed songs get the video link on the board.

Once a song enters the pick chain (“Up next”), its vote box locks — no more votes, no withdrawing, and the sender can no longer delete it. This protects the votes everyone (including people who bought them) already put in.

How does the Daily Spin work?

2 free spins a day per account and per device, resetting at 00:00 (GMT+7). The wheel has 16 equal segments, and the segment size you see is the real probability:

Spin rewards go to a separate Bonus balance (spent before purchased votes). Spins are free — nothing is charged or deducted to play.

How do you keep voting and spins fair?

All limits live in the database and in an edge shield, not in the interface: per-account and per-device limits on spins, a fingerprint/IP cap that blocks farms, one vote ledger per account, and vote totals that only change through one audited function. The board also groups duplicate requests so one song's votes aren't split across copies.

What we store and why is in the Privacy page.

Do I have to pay for anything?

No. Requests, votes, spins, comments, streaks and the leaderboard are all usable for free every day. Paying only buys convenience: more votes, or skipping the queue with a paid request.
