Privacy

Last updated: 22/09/2026

This page collects the minimum needed to run a request board and a ranking. It states what we store, why, and what you can ask us to change.

1. What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive and store only:

  • Display name from your Google account
  • Public avatar
  • Email address, used to identify the account

We also store what you create: requests, votes, comments and replies, vote orders with their payment status, achievement claims, and Daily Spin records (account, time, request ID, reward and hashed browser, fingerprint and IP identifiers).

The site also records one activity date per account for each request, vote, comment or spin. Raw activity dates are account-scoped; public profiles expose only aggregate current/longest streaks and unlocked streak milestones. Activity dates are used for streaks and achievements, not for advertising or cross-site tracking.

We do not collect: passwords, contacts, mailbox contents, Drive files, calendar, location, or anything else in your Google account. The only scopes we request are name, picture and email.

2. What it is used for

  • Recognising you when you sign in
  • Showing your name and avatar next to your requests and on the leaderboard
  • Applying the per-account limits on requests and votes, and the shared browser/account daily spin limits; preventing duplicate bonus claims
  • Matching a bank transfer to your order

We do not use your data for advertising, behavioural analysis or marketing email.

3. Third parties

We do not sell, rent or trade your data. It sits on the two services that run the site:

  • Supabase — database and sign-in
  • Cloudflare Pages — where the site is hosted

4. What is public

Your display name and avatar appear next to your requests and on the leaderboard. Song, artist, request type, link and note fields on a request, plus public comments and replies, can be read by visitors so the board can work. Public profiles show approved request summaries, received-vote totals, aggregate streaks and streak milestones. Your email address, votes cast for other people, raw activity dates, raw spin rewards and browser/fingerprint/IP hashes are never shown publicly. You can change your display name and avatar at any time in your profile. Do not put private information in a request, note or comment.

5. Payments

The page does not process cards and stores no financial details. You pay by your own bank transfer or PayPal, an admin confirms it manually, and we keep only the amount, the vote count and the order status.

6. Retention

Data is kept while your account exists. When you ask us to delete it, the profile, requests, votes, comments, achievement ledger rows and orders are removed permanently. Spin records are kept for bonus accounting, duplicate-claim prevention and device limits. Deleting an account removes its account association from those records, but does not restore already-used device spins. Contact us with questions or a request concerning these device records.

7. Your rights

  • View — requests and orders in "My requests", today’s rewards in "Daily Spin"; contact us for other stored records
  • Correct — name and avatar in your profile
  • Delete parts — remove your own requests, cancel unconfirmed orders
  • Delete everything — message us on Telegram
  • Revoke access — remove this app on the Google permissions page

8. Cookies and local storage

No advertising cookies and no third-party analytics. The browser's local storage keeps your session, display and sound preferences.

Daily Spin browser identifier. When you open Daily Spin while signed in, we save a random server-issued token in local storage (ccl.spin.device.v1) and a first-party cookie (ccl_spin_device_v1). The cookie lasts up to one year and is renewed when used; the local-storage copy remains until site data is removed. The database stores only the token’s hash, not the raw token.

This identifier is separate from your login, so signing out or switching accounts does not grant extra spins. If just one copy is removed, the other copy restores it. We also keep an unconfirmed spin’s request ID locally so retrying cannot claim a reward twice.

This identifier is not hardware fingerprinting and is never used to track you across websites. Another browser, private browsing, or clearing all site data may look like a new device. The account limit still applies when you use the same account. Disabling local storage may prevent spinning.

Daily Spin fraud shield (only on deployments with our Cloudflare edge gate). To stop automated farming on shared networks, the spin page additionally computes a browser fingerprint with the open-source FingerprintJS library and sends its SHA-256 hash, together with a Cloudflare Turnstile check, to our edge gate with each spin. The gate also reads your IP address from the connection. It stores only short-lived counters in Cloudflare KV, keyed by the fingerprint hash and by IP, which expire at midnight (GMT+7); at most five different fingerprints may spin from one IP per day, and an IP exceeding that is blocked until the next day. The database keeps only the fingerprint hash and a hash of the IP alongside each spin for abuse investigations — never the raw IP or a reversible fingerprint. None of this is shared with advertisers or used outside Daily Spin.

9. Children

The page is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated.
